    <h2 id="research-title">Rigorous learning systems, grounded in the real world.</h2>
    <p>My work connects trustworthy machine learning with the constraints, uncertainty, and limited observations that define physical environments.</p>
  </div>

      <ul class="research-card__topics">
        <li>Safe reinforcement learning</li>
        <li>Off-policy evaluation</li>

      <ul class="research-card__topics">

        <li>Probabilistic LoRA</li>
